## 6. Mapping judgment — fields → the four coupling-factor indicators

The latent contract-exposure factor `C*` (§5.6) is to be measured by four indicators with independent measurement error. What this EDGAR pass can and cannot proxy:

### Indicator 3 — vendor-financing share of capex → **MEASURED (partially)**
Directly observable numerator and denominator components:
- Anthropic: $26.0B vendor equity placement (Google/NVIDIA/AMD/Broadcom/Micron/Microsoft/TSMC) against $900.9B compute commitments and $18.8B 9M-2026 compute opex. Vendor placement ≈ 2.9% of committed spend — but note the placement is equity into the *buyer* of compute, i.e. suppliers pre-funding their own customer's purchases (circularity leg).
- CoreWeave: NVIDIA $2.0B equity against $23.6B 9M-2026 capex (≈8.5% one-quarter-partial basis) + OEM financing arrangements (undisclosed size).
- IREN: $3,645M GPU-specific secured financing against $18,947M capital commitments (≈19%).
- APLD: CoreWeave warrants (equity kicker to tenant) — customer-financing direction reversed; still a coupling leg.
- WULF: Google backstop + $515.5M warrant consideration + $3.2B secured notes against Fluidstack/Anthropic leases.
**Caveat:** share-of-capex ratios need consistent period alignment (commitments are multi-year stocks; placements are flows). Computable as a panel entry per firm-quarter from these filings.

### Indicator 4 — backlog from partly-capitalised counterparties → **MEASURED (partially)**
RPO is disclosed for Anthropic ($13.8B), CoreWeave ($132.3B), IREN ($6,347M). "Partly-capitalised counterparty" resolution is limited by anonymized customer labels (A/B/C):
- CoreWeave's $132.3B RPO sits against Customers A/B (33%/26% of 9M revenue) — identity verifiable only from press, not the filing → **partly UNMEASURED** at filing level.
- TeraWulf's Anthropic HPC Leases (401 MW, not yet commenced at 9/30) were contracted with Anthropic *while private* — a textbook partly-capitalised counterparty; now post-IPO Anthropic is capitalised via this same EDGAR record. MW-denominated, no $ RPO → dollarization **UNMEASURED** from filings.
- IREN RPO is dominated by Microsoft (fully capitalised) — negative case, useful as control.

### Indicator 1 — `η_s,C` (capital-supply elasticity) → **UNMEASURED directly**
Filings disclose *levels* of capital-supply response to demand expectations (Anthropic $900.9B commitments; CoreWeave $23.6B capex + $47.5B debt; IREN commitments +$5.1B QoQ), not price-quantity elasticities. These levels are the correct *inputs* to a firm-quarter panel from which `η_s,C` can later be estimated (per §5.6's panel caveat), but no single filing yields an elasticity.

### Indicator 2 — `η_d,R` (resource-demand elasticity) → **UNMEASURED**
Revenue/run-rate disclosures (Anthropic 9M-2026 $33.2B, RR ≈$81B; CoreWeave 9M $8.3B; etc.) are demand levels at observed prices. No filing discloses price variation sufficient to identify a demand elasticity for compute resources. Anthropic's consumption-based pricing disclosure ("customers may reduce or cease usage at any point") and CSP-channel share (44%) are qualitative demand-flexibility evidence only.
